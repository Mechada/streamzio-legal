Controla la interacción de tus directos desde tu PC.

Streamzio · Desktop application

Manage live-stream interactions from your PC.

Streamzio is a Windows desktop application project for organizing game actions, presets, plugins and overlays based on TikTok LIVE interactions.

Status: development and private testing. Some integrations and features are not yet available.

Your Streamzio account is separate from the public TikTok channel you select. Third-party integrations may depend on permissions, review or external service availability.
